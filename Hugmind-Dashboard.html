Principles & Red Flags
Rules to Live By + Warning Signs

8 Guiding Principles

1. Revenue solves most problems.
2. Speed beats perfection.
3. Constraints are features.
4. Feedback is free consulting.
5. Network is net worth.
6. Document or die.
7. Cash is oxygen (3+ months runway).
8. Founders set the pace.

30-Day Coma Test: Could this run 30 days without founders? Passes → scale. Fails → fix or accept. Goal: core ops pass by Month 12.

Red Flags

Sales: No new conversations, proposals without follow-up, discounting before selling value, "too busy with delivery."

Delivery: Missed deadlines normalised, multiple revision rounds, Luis reviewing everything manually, Ravi waiting for direction.

Financial: Revenue flat 2+ months, margin shrinking, overdue invoices growing, spending before profitable.

Founder: One person doing 80%, avoiding hard conversations, building tools instead of selling.
